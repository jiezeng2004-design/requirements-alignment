## Requirements Alignment Auto Mode

- For non-trivial work, inspect the repository and context before deciding whether direction alignment is needed.
- Invoke `$requirements-alignment` for greenfield projects, blank repositories, new products or tools, vague ideas, or tasks where the product goal, MVP boundary, primary interaction, or another direction-defining decision is unresolved.
- A reversible, low-risk, or common implementation default is not a reason to make an unresolved product decision for the user.
- For existing projects and explicit implementation tasks, remain conservative: do not invoke it for mechanical edits, formatting, renames, clear bug fixes, safe local implementation details, or facts established by the repository.
- Ask only the highest-value direction questions, then continue as soon as the product direction is clear enough to implement.
